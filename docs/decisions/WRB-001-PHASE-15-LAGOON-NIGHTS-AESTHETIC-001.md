## Scope

This lock applies to the `Lagoon Nights — Moonlit Waterfront` environment currently connected off Explore Brevard Main Street.

It does **not** lock all future Explore Brevard streets or districts into this same atmosphere.

## Locked visual direction

- nighttime waterfront / lagoon atmosphere;
- large dramatic moon rising low over the water;
- strong moon reflection across the water;
- minimal buildings;
- minimal people;
- open negative space and long sightlines;
- clean futuristic-coastal architecture;
- restrained lighting rather than dense commercial signage;
- tropical / Space Coast cues that read Brevard without becoming a tourism collage;
- polished, premium, cinematic presentation;
- same world family as Main Street, but a clearly different mood;
- no storm-damage, ruined-town, or cluttered disaster aesthetic.

## Brevard cues

- lagoon / coastal water;
- palms and subtropical landscaping;
- docks / sailboats / waterfront promenade;
- subtle causeway / bridge language;
- distant Space Coast / launch-complex silhouettes;
- open Florida horizon and reflected sky.
