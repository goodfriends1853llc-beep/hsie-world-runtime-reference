# Founding Business — Standard Messages

## A. First response / intake message

You're in the right place. I built one intake so every founding business goes through the same process.

Fill this out:

https://goodfriends1853llc-beep.github.io/hsie-world-runtime-reference/tommie/founding-intake.html

It asks for your business, what you want visitors to be able to do, the kind of environment you want, and what assets you already have. This is still the public pilot stage, so the world will keep growing, but I am standardizing the process now.

Nothing gets published as your approved business representation until you review it first. Once I get your intake, I turn it into a build brief and send you the next step.

## B. Intake received

I got your Explore Brevard intake.

I’m reviewing your business, the action you want customers to take, your visual direction, and the assets you have available. If anything important is missing, I’ll send you one list instead of asking questions one at a time.

Next step after that is your build brief.

[INSERT ONLY THE MISSING ITEMS]

## D. Build brief ready

I turned your intake into the build brief.

Before I build, I need you to check four things:
1. how your business is described;
2. the environment direction;
3. what visitors should be able to do;
4. anything that must not be shown.

Once you approve the brief, I build against that version instead of guessing from our messages.

## E. Preview ready

Your first Explore Brevard preview is ready.

Check the business name, visuals, atmosphere, wording, buttons/icons, and where every action sends the visitor. Send all changes together if you can.

This is the review version — it is not treated as your approved public representation until you approve it.
