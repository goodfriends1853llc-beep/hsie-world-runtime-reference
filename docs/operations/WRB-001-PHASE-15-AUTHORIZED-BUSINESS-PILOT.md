# WRB-001 — Phase 15 Authorized Business Pilot

**Status:** OPEN / PILOT SUBJECT REQUIRED

## Mission

Move from the fictional/sample Lagoon Cuts proof into one real, explicitly authorized business pilot without expanding the claim beyond what has actually been built.

## Pilot minimum

The first real pilot requires exactly one authorized business.

Before any real-business activation, record:

1. business display name;
2. authorized representative name or stable identifier;
3. confirmation that the representative authorizes the business to appear in the WRB-001 spatial experience;
4. approved public business description;
5. approved services or public actions;
6. approved booking/call/site destination, if used;
7. approved logo/photos/assets and permission to use them;
8. target World Place / Space representation;
9. whether the business is physical, mobile, service-area, online, or hybrid;
10. the exact pilot start status.

## Required evidence states

`INTEREST ≠ AUTHORIZATION`

`AUTHORIZATION ≠ ACTIVATION`

`ACTIVATION ≠ PUBLICATION`

`PUBLICATION ≠ COMMERCIAL PERFORMANCE`

The pilot must preserve those distinctions.

## Governed activation path

For the real pilot, the intended sequence is:

`Business intake → authorization evidence → public-content approval → Place/Representation preparation → deterministic snapshot → governed ACTIVATE_BUSINESS_PLACE → MIA receipt/proof → World Runtime receipt → static-publication update → external mobile check`

The current public GitHub Pages surface remains static. A governed local activation does not automatically change the public site.

## Pilot claim ceiling

A successful Phase-15 pilot may establish only that one authorized real business was represented and activated through the bounded WRB-001 pilot flow.

It does not establish broad production readiness, business results, guaranteed bookings, traffic, sales, or fitness for every business.
