PRIVACY NOTICE.

This public pilot collects only information a person chooses to submit through contact, booking, or founding-business forms.

What may be collected

Name, business/project name, email, optional phone number, business information, links, asset references, and submitted messages or intake details.

How it is used

To respond to inquiries, review founding-business requests, prepare build briefs, and manage requested pilot work.

Form delivery

Forms are currently delivered to Tommie by email through a third-party form service. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, private customer records, or information you are not authorized to share.

Public representation

Submitting business information does not make it public automatically. A founding-business representation requires review and approval before it is treated as an approved public business representation.
